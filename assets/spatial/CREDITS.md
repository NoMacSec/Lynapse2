# lynapse-kemar.wav

Lynapse's default spatial-audio HRIR (14-channel HeSuVi layout, 48 kHz, 32-bit float), generated by `dsp/make_hrir.py`.

Built from **"HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR Dummy-Head Microphone"** by **Bill Gardner and Keith Martin**,
MIT Media Lab, 1994 — https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html

> This data is Copyright 1994 by the MIT Media Laboratory. It is provided free with no restrictions on use,
> provided the authors are cited when the data is used in any research or commercial application.

Processing added by Lynapse (diffuse-field EQ, 48 kHz resampling, synthetic room tail, level matching) is described in
`dsp/make_hrir.py` and can be reproduced with `python3 dsp/make_hrir.py lynapse-kemar.wav`.

You can use any other 14-channel HeSuVi-format HRIR instead (only files you are allowed to use; they are not shipped).
